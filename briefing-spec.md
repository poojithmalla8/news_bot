# Daily Briefing Spec

The morning brief follows these sections in this order. Keep each item to one
line with the key fact; add a short source link where it matters. Skip a
section only if there is genuinely nothing to report (rare).

## Subject / title

`Your Morning News Brief — <Weekday>, <Month D>` (e.g. "Your Morning News Brief — Sunday, October 4")

## Sections

### 1. 🇺🇸 US Top News (10–20 items)
- The day's biggest US stories.
- Always include: election-related news, newly passed/signed government bills.
- Always include: anything that could impact an H1B visa holder (immigration
  policy, USCIS/DOL rule changes, visa bulletin, layoffs affecting status, etc.).

### 2. 📈 Economics & Markets
- Previous close / current state of S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow (values + % change).
- Key movers and why; Fed/interest-rate/inflation headlines; any major
  economic data released or due today.

### 3. 🏏⚽ Sports
- Top cricket headlines (international, IPL when in season).
- Top football headlines (soccer + American football as relevant).

### 4. 🌤 Weather — 95134 (San Jose, CA)
- Today's forecast: high/low, conditions, any alerts.
- Note: follow the user's current location if it changes.

### 5. 🌍 World Politics (5–10 items)
- Top global political developments.

### 6. ⚠️ Natural Calamities
- Earthquakes, hurricanes/typhoons, floods, wildfires, and other disasters
  worldwide from the last 24–48h.

### 7. 🇮🇳 India News (15–20 items)
- Politics, policy, economy.
- Markets: Sensex/Nifty movement, notable stocks.
- IPOs: new filings, listings, allotment/GMP buzz.
- Other major stories.

## Tone

Concise and skimmable. Facts first, no fluff. Flag clearly when a number
(price, poll figure, bill status) could not be verified.
